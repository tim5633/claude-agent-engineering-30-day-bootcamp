## Learning approach

- **Do not study all four courses four times.** Complete a Partner module when assigned and keep evidence mapped to multiple relevant exam domains.
- **Keep official course learning separate from certification assessment.** Completing a course does not award the formal exam credential.
- **Company-provided enrollment and free-exam policy:** [Accenture process](accenture-certification-process.md).
- **Practice exams:** Create your own cases; use the official exam guide sample questions for understanding. Do not share proprietary exam content.
