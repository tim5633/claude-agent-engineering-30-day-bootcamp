> English first · [繁體中文](#traditional-chinese) · Based on the Accenture message supplied by the learner (dated 9 October 2026), cross-checked against public Anthropic Partner Academy certification pages.

## Four company-announced credentials

| Code | Official credential | Focus | Suggested readiness |
|---|---|---|---|
| CCAO-F | [Associate – Foundations](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/claude-certified-associate-foundations-certification) | Professional Claude workflow, prompting, evaluation, governance | Day 17–22 |
| CCDV-F | [Developer – Foundations](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications) | Claude Code, API, MCP, production agents and evaluations | Day 21–23 |
| CCAR-F | [Architect – Foundations](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/claude-certified-architect-foundations-certification) | Agent architecture, MCP/tool design, Claude Code, context | Day 27–29 |
| CCAR-P | [Architect – Professional](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications) | Enterprise design, integration, safety, stakeholder delivery | Day 30+ |

These are **independent, separate exams**, not automatically unlocked or awarded after completing the course. Sitting all four within 30 days is an optional stretch goal, not a condition for completing the bootcamp. Architect Professional requires extra review if readiness is insufficient.

## Exact process shown in the internal message

1. **Register in Workday FIRST** using the internal links in the original company message. The screenshot says this is needed to charge eligible examination and learning-path study time to the central training WBSE.
2. **Register in [Anthropic Partner Academy](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/)** using the Accenture company email.
3. **Download the exam guide for each selected certification** via the [official partner certifications page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications). Treat guide domains, sample questions and explanations as the main preparation resource.
4. **Review the exam guide and follow recommended learning paths** inside Partner Academy. Lessons are mapped to the [30 daily curriculum](../README.md#complete-30-day-curriculum).
5. **Schedule through Pearson VUE** online or at a testing centre once the exam is registered and you are ready. Follow links shown in your company training portal / Partner Academy.

### Company fee deadline — verify at checkout

The internal Accenture post dated **9 October 2026** states that all four exam attempts **including retakes** are free through **31 December 2026**. Anthropic's public FAQ confirms 100% exam discounts for Global Premier partners through that date, contingent on a recognized company email domain. Pricing and retake eligibility must still be confirmed in the official registration checkout and company policy. Do **not** assume free exams continue after 31 December.

If a company domain is not recognized, see [Anthropic Partner FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications). Do not register with a personal email.

# Accenture Claude 官方認證：公司申請流程與應試計畫

> 本文件根據你提供的 **2026 年 10 月 9 日** Accenture 內部公告，以及 Anthropic 公開認證頁面整理。原始公司內部網址、WBSE 與個資不公開。

## 公司提供的四張認證

| 代碼 | 官方認證 | 核心能力 | 建議準備時段 |
|---|---|---|---|
| CCAO-F | [Associate – Foundations](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/claude-certified-associate-foundations-certification) | Claude 工作流程、Prompt、驗證、治理 | Day 17–22 |
| CCDV-F | [Developer – Foundations](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications) | Claude Code、API、MCP、Production Agents、Evals | Day 21–23 |
| CCAR-F | [Architect – Foundations](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/claude-certified-architect-foundations-certification) | Agent 架構、MCP、Context、工具設計 | Day 27–29 |
| CCAR-P | [Architect – Professional](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications) | 企業架構、整合、安全、客戶溝通 | Day 30 及後續 |

四張是**不同的正式考試**，不會因為完課自動取得；一個月全部考完是選修挑戰，不是完成這份計畫的必要條件。

## 公司公告指定的五步驟

1. **先在 Workday 登記**（一定要第一步）。公司公告說明相關考試／課程可依規定計入中央訓練 WBSE。
2. 以 **Accenture 公司 Email** 註冊 [Anthropic Partner Academy](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/)。
3. 在 [官方認證頁](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications)下載每張 **Exam Guide**。它包含考綱、Sample Questions 與解釋，是最重要的備考基準。
4. 複習 Exam Guide，依照 [每天的課程](../README.md#完整-30-天課表)完成 Partner Academy 指定模組與實作。
5. 準備好後從正式流程預約 **Pearson VUE**，選擇線上或實體考場。

### 免費期限與注意事項

根據公司 **2026/10/09** 公告，四張考試及 Retakes **免費至 2026/12/31**。Anthropic 公開 FAQ 亦確認符合資格 Global Premier Partner 在該日期前享有 100% 考試折扣，但最終請以你用公司信箱登入後的**實際報考費用、重考規則和公司政策**為準。

## 建議認證安排

- **Day 01：** 完成 Workday 登記、公司信箱 Partner Academy 註冊、下載四份 Exam Guides。
- **Day 02–17：** 透過 Daily Plan 吸收 Associate 七大模組，並學好 Developer 基礎。
- **Day 18–22：** Associate／Developer Exam Readiness，完成實作和限時自測。
- **Day 23–27：** Architect Foundations 五大考綱與架構情境訓練。
- **Day 28–30：** Architect Professional 五大模組與企業級架構答辯。
- **12/31 前：** 視準備程度和考位安排正式考試／可用的重考機會。
